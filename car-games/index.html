Free car games for kids

Car and truck games for little kids who love anything with wheels. Smash toy cars with a friendly dinosaur, scrub muddy cars clean at the car wash, or drive diggers, dump trucks and steamrollers around a building site.

Try Chomper's Car Wash - a calm toddler favourite - plus Chomper's Car Crunch, Chomper's Digger and Busy Building Site.

All free to play online, with big touch controls that work on phones and tablets as well as computers.

Questions parents ask

What car games are there?

Chomper's Car Crunch (stomp and crunch cartoon cars as a friendly dinosaur), Chomper's Car Wash (scrub, rinse, paint and add stickers), plus the diggers and trucks in Busy Building Site.

Which is easiest for little ones?

Chomper's Car Crunch is for ages 2 and up - just drag Chomper around. The car wash and building site suit 3 and up.

Are they free to play?

Yes - free, no ads, no sign-up, and they play in the browser on phones, tablets and computers.
