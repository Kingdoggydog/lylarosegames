Free dinosaur games for kids

Dinosaur games starring Chomper, a cute purple dinosaur who is never scary. Chomper crunches toy cars, drives a steam train, washes muddy cars, digs for dinosaur bones and delivers cargo on a toy train set.

Play Chomper's Car Crunch, Chomper's Choo-Choo Express, Chomper's Car Wash, Chomper's Digger, Chomper's Toot Toot Delivery, Chomper's Digger Maze, or take the animals for a ride in Chomper's Animal Train, made for toddlers.

These dino games suit toddlers and preschoolers - simple controls, lots of roaring, and no game-over screens. Free to play online on any device.

Questions parents ask

Are the dinosaur games scary?

No - the star is Chomper, a friendly purple dinosaur who drives trains, washes cars and digs for treasure. Nothing chases or scares anyone.

Which dinosaur games suit 2 year olds?

Chomper's Car Crunch, Chomper's Choo-Choo Express and Chomper's Animal Train are the easiest, with big taps and nothing to lose.

What about older dinosaur fans?

Try Chomper's Car Wash, Chomper's Digger or the train puzzle Chomper's Toot Toot Delivery. All free, no ads.
