What is Sunny Pals?

Sunny Pals: Games for Kids 2-6 is the app version of this website. It has the same 27 gentle games - trains, diggers, puppies, unicorns, mazes and more - packed into one app that works anywhere, even with no internet.

The website stays free, just as it is. The app is for times the browser doesn't suit: a long drive, a flight, a waiting room with no wifi, or when you'd rather your child had a proper full-screen app with no browser bar to tap by accident.

Four promises

  • 🚫No adsNot one. No pop-ups, no videos to sit through, nothing selling to your kids.
  • 🔒No trackingThe app collects no data at all. No accounts, no analytics, nothing leaves the device.
  • ✈️No wifi neededEvery game is inside the app, so it plays the same in the car, on a plane or at the beach.
  • 💜One-off unlockOne small purchase opens everything - no subscription, ever. Family Sharing is on.

What's free, and what's unlocked

These 4 games are free to play forever, so your child can try the app properly first:

  • 🦄 Glitter Sky Unicorn
  • 🚁 Whirlybird Rescue
  • 🫧 Lenny's Bubble Pop
  • 🚂 Chomper's Choo-Choo Express

One purchase (A$3.99 in Australia) unlocks all 27 games, plus every new game we add later. With Family Sharing, one purchase covers the whole family's iPhones and iPads.

The purchase, and anything else meant for grown-ups, sits behind a simple maths question, so little ones can't buy anything or wander off by accident.

How it started

Every game in Sunny Pals began with my daughter Lyla saying "Dad, can you make a game where...?" A unicorn flying through a glittery sky. A puppy who needs a bubbly bath. A train full of animals. I'm a dad in Melbourne, so I made them for her, and she and her little brother test every single one before it goes anywhere.

They're the kind of games I wanted my own kids to have: gentle, a bit silly, and with nothing designed to keep them hooked. If your kids enjoy them too, that makes our day.

Privacy

Sunny Pals collects no personal information and no usage data of any kind - not even anonymous visit counts. It's in Apple's Kids category, which has Apple's strictest rules. Read the full Sunny Pals privacy policy.
