Free puzzle games for kids

These free puzzle games give young kids something to think about without any pressure. There are jigsaw puzzles with big friendly pieces, a memory card matching game, a toy train set where you switch the tracks to deliver the cargo, and a set of maze games.

Blossom's Jigsaw Puzzles start with just 4 pieces and go up to 12, with a faint picture to help. Lenny's Memory Match is a classic flip-two memory game with jungle pictures. Chomper's Toot Toot Delivery is a gentle logic puzzle where kids work out which way to switch the tracks. For toddlers, Blossom's Shape Sorter is a first puzzle: drag the chunky shapes into their matching holes, just like the wooden toy.

Puzzles help with memory, shapes, matching and thinking ahead. Everything is free, plays in the browser on tablets, phones and computers, and saves progress on your device.

Questions parents ask

Which puzzle game is easiest?

Blossom's Shape Sorter (2+) works like the wooden toy - drag each shape into its hole. Then try Lenny's Memory Match and Blossom's Jigsaw Puzzles.

What do kids practise in puzzle games?

Shapes and colours, memory, fitting pieces together and thinking ahead - Chomper's Toot Toot Delivery is all about planning which switches to flick.

Are the puzzles free?

Yes - free to play in the browser, with no ads, no sign-up and nothing to download.
