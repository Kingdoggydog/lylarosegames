Christmas games for kids

Cosy Christmas fun - trees to decorate, a unicorn sleigh ride, a snowy maze and an advent calendar.

Free Christmas games for kids

Our Christmas games are on their way! We're making a little set of cosy, gentle Christmas games for kids aged about 2 to 6, and they'll start appearing here in late November - in time to play all through December.

What's coming

Blossom's Christmas Tree - decorate a Christmas tree with Blossom the Bunnycorn: baubles, tinsel, lights and a star on top, then switch it on and watch it sparkle. Big taps for toddlers, no reading needed.
Rainbow's Christmas Sleigh - Rainbow the flying unicorn pulls Santa's sleigh across a snowy night sky, dropping presents down the chimneys.
Christmas Maze - deliver presents around a snowy little village.
Advent calendar - 24 doors, one to open each day from 1 December, each with a little surprise inside.

Until then

Little ones can fly through the clouds in Glitter Sky Unicorn, sort shapes in Blossom's Shape Sorter or help the animals aboard Chomper's Animal Train. All our games are free, with no ads and no sign-up - see the toddler games and unicorn games pages for more.

Questions parents ask

When will the Christmas games be ready?

The first ones arrive in late November, and the advent calendar opens its first door on 1 December. This page fills up as each game is finished.

Are the Christmas games free?

Yes. Like every game on Lyla Rose Games, they are free to play in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with no ads, no in-game shop and no sign-up.

Will there be Christmas games for toddlers?

Yes - Blossom's Christmas Tree is made for 2 and 3 year olds, with big taps, nothing to read and no way to lose. The sleigh ride and the maze are for about 3 and up.

What is in the advent calendar?

Twenty-four doors, one for each day from 1 to 24 December. Each opens a little surprise - a picture, a short animation or a tiny game - so there's something new to look forward to every day.
