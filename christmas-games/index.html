Free Christmas games for kids

Cosy, gentle Christmas games for kids aged about 2 to 6 - free to play online, with no ads and no sign-up. More are arriving through November, in time to play all through December.

Decorate a Christmas tree

In Blossom's Christmas Tree, little ones pick a tree - classic green, snowy white, pastel pink or a tiny tabletop one - and decorate it with Blossom the Bunnycorn: baubles, tinsel, fairy lights, candy canes, gingerbread people and a star (or a unicorn horn!) on top. Flick the big switch and the room goes cosy and dim, the tree twinkles, presents slide in underneath and each one pops open with a toy. It's made for toddlers - big taps, no reading and no way to lose - and the tree stays saved on your device.

Questions parents ask

When will all the Christmas games be ready?

Blossom's Christmas Tree is ready to play now. The sleigh ride and the maze arrive in late November, and the advent calendar opens its first door on 1 December. This page fills up as each game is finished.

Are the Christmas games free?

Yes. Like every game on Lyla Rose Games, they are free to play in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with no ads, no in-game shop and no sign-up.

Will there be Christmas games for toddlers?

Yes - Blossom's Christmas Tree is made for 2 and 3 year olds, with big taps, nothing to read and no way to lose. The sleigh ride and the maze are for about 3 and up.

What is in the advent calendar?

Twenty-four doors, one for each day from 1 to 24 December. Each opens a little surprise - a picture, a short animation or a tiny game - so there's something new to look forward to every day.
