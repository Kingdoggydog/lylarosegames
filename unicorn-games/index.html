Free unicorn games for kids

Unicorn games full of rainbows, glitter and soft pastel colours, made for little kids who love all things magical. Meet Starlight the rainbow-maned unicorn, Rainbow the flying unicorn, Sparklehoof and Blossom the Bunnycorn - and watch out for cheeky Lenny the Lemur! Every game is free to play online, with no ads, no sign-up and nothing to download.

Unicorn games for toddlers (ages 2 and 3)

The littlest unicorn fans can post shapes into Blossom's toy box in Blossom's Shape Sorter decorate a Christmas tree in Blossom's Christmas Tree or make silly pumpkins in Blossom's Pumpkin Faces - just taps, no reading and no way to lose. From about three, they can fly through the clouds catching glitter in Glitter Sky Unicorn, catch eggs for Blossom's friends in Blossom's Easter Eggs, gallop over hurdles in Starlight's Unicorn Race and finish bright pictures in Blossom's Jigsaw Puzzles.

Unicorn games for 4 to 6 year olds

Bigger kids get a bit more challenge: Unicorn Pinball has flippers made of unicorn horns, Unicorn Penalty Shoot-out asks how many goals in a row they can score past Lenny, Glitter Getaway is a rainbow-bridge runner, and Sparklehoof's Maze is a hedge maze with stars to collect.

Gentle by design

These are calm, friendly games for preschoolers - lots of cheering and very little losing, no scary bits, no pop-ups and no chat. Most have music and sound buttons so you can turn them down, and they work on a phone, tablet or computer. For more like these, try our toddler games or games for 4 year olds.

Questions parents ask

Are these unicorn games really free?

Yes. Every unicorn game here is free to play online, with no ads, no in-game shop and no sign-up. Nothing your child does is collected.

Which unicorn games suit 3 year olds?

Glitter Sky Unicorn, Starlight's Unicorn Race, Blossom's Easter Eggs and Blossom's Jigsaw Puzzles. For 2 year olds there's Blossom's Shape Sorter.

Are there unicorn games for older kids?

Yes - Unicorn Pinball, Unicorn Penalty Shoot-out, Glitter Getaway and Sparklehoof's Maze are for 4 and up.

Can kids play the unicorn games on an iPad or phone?

Yes - they play straight in the web browser on iPads, tablets, phones and computers. On a phone or tablet you can add the site to the home screen so it opens like an app.

Who is Blossom the Bunnycorn?

A bunny with a unicorn horn - she stars in the shape sorter, jigsaws, Easter egg hunt and the pumpkin game.
