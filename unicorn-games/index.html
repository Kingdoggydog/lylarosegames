Free unicorn games for kids

Unicorn games full of rainbows, glitter and sparkles, made for little kids who love all things magical. Meet Starlight the rainbow-maned unicorn, Rainbow the flying unicorn, Sparklehoof and Blossom the Bunnycorn - and watch out for cheeky Lenny the Lemur!

Fly through the sky in Glitter Sky Unicorn, score goals in the Unicorn Penalty Shoot-out, race on sports day in Starlight's Unicorn Race, play Unicorn Pinball with flippers made of unicorn horns, find the way through Sparklehoof's Maze, hunt for Easter eggs with Blossom, or make silly Halloween pumpkin faces with her in Blossom's Pumpkin Faces. The littlest players can post shapes into her toy box in Blossom's Shape Sorter.

All of our unicorn games are free to play online with no ads and no sign-up. They work on phones, tablets and computers, and they're gentle enough for preschoolers - lots of cheering, very little losing.

Questions parents ask

Which unicorn games suit 3 year olds?

Glitter Sky Unicorn, Starlight's Unicorn Race, Blossom's Easter Eggs and Blossom's Jigsaw Puzzles. For 2 year olds there's Blossom's Shape Sorter.

Who is Blossom the Bunnycorn?

A bunny with a unicorn horn - she stars in the shape sorter, jigsaws, Easter egg hunt and the pumpkin game.

Are there unicorn games for older kids?

Yes - Unicorn Pinball, Unicorn Penalty Shoot-out, Glitter Getaway and Sparklehoof's Maze are for 4 and up. All free, no ads.
