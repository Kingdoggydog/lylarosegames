About Blossom's Easter Eggs

Blossom's Easter Eggs is a free Easter game for kids aged about 3 and up - and it was Lyla's own idea. Blossom the Bunnycorn catches falling Easter eggs (but not the stinky rotten ones!) and then delivers the colours each animal friend wished for.

It mixes catching with colour matching, and there's no game over. Lovely around Easter, but fun any time of year.

Free, no ads and no sign-up. More like this: unicorn games and animal games.
