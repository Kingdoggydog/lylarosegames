About Biscuit's Puppy Care

Biscuit's Puppy Care is a free puppy game for toddlers aged about 2 and up. Little ones look after Biscuit, a fluffy apricot cavoodle, by tapping big picture buttons: fill his bowl, give him a bubbly bath, brush the leaves out of his curls, dress him up and tuck him into his basket for a sleep.

There's no reading, no timer and nothing to lose - every tap gets a happy sound, a helping hand shows what to do next, and the routine loops round for as long as they like. Biscuit's outfit is saved on this device.

Free, no ads and no sign-up. More like this: animal games and puzzle games.
