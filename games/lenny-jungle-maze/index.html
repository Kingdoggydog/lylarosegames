About Lenny's Jungle Maze

Lenny's Jungle Maze is a free jungle maze game for kids aged about 3 and up. Kids help Lenny the Lemur find the lost baby lemurs hiding in the jungle maze. Each baby joins a wobbly line behind him, and he leads them all home to his treehouse. Vine swings give shortcuts across the river.

Every level can be finished without the vines, a toucan hint shows the way, and paw prints show where Lenny has been - so nobody gets stuck. Six levels with bananas to collect, and no timer or losing.

Free, no ads and no sign-up. More like this: maze games and animal games.
