About Rainbow's Christmas Sleigh

Rainbow's Christmas Sleigh is a free unicorn Christmas game for kids aged about 3 and up. Rainbow the flying unicorn pulls Santa's sleigh over five snowy villages, and children tap the big Drop button to send presents down the glowing chimneys so each house lights up.

It gently practises timing and hand-eye coordination. Presents drift towards the nearest chimney, and a missed one is caught by a friendly snowman, so there are no lives and no losing. Each night takes about a minute, stars are saved on this device, and it works best held sideways.

Free, no ads and no sign-up. More like this: Christmas games and unicorn games.
