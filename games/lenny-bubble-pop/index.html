About Lenny's Bubble Pop

Lenny's Bubble Pop is a free bubble popping game for toddlers and kids aged about 2 and up. Lenny the Lemur blows big, slow, shiny bubbles and little ones simply tap to pop them. Some hide a surprise - a fish, a butterfly, a star, a banana for Lenny to munch, or a baby lemur who swings away on a vine.

It's built for first taps: one finger, no reading, no timer and nothing to lose. Ten pops fill the row at the top, then a giant bubble brings a confetti party and a new place - the beach, bath time, the night sky or under the sea.

Free, no ads and no sign-up. More like this: animal games and action games.
