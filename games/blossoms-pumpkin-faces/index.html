About Blossom's Pumpkin Faces

Blossom's Pumpkin Faces is a free Halloween pumpkin game for kids aged about 2 and up, made with toddlers in mind. Children pick a pumpkin from Blossom the Bunnycorn's patch, tap on eyes, noses, mouths and silly extras like a witch hat or a moustache, then light it up and watch it giggle, dance and sing.

It's pumpkin carving with no knives and nothing scary: every face is friendly, there's no reading, no timer and no losing. Finished pumpkins line Blossom's porch steps and stay saved on this device.
