About Biscuit's Fetch

Biscuit's Fetch is a free puppy game for toddlers aged about 2 and up. One tap anywhere in the park throws the ball, and Biscuit, a bouncy apricot cavoodle, races off to fetch it. Sometimes he brings the ball back - and sometimes a shoe, a rubber duck, a sandwich or a stick much too big for him.

There's nothing to read, nothing to lose and no timer, so it suits very little hands. Tapping further away throws further, which gently teaches cause and effect. Every eighth throw there's a tummy-tickle party and the park changes - beach, snow, autumn leaves.

Free, no ads and no sign-up. More like this: animal games and action games.
