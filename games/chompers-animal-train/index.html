About Chomper's Animal Train

Chomper's Animal Train is a free train game for toddlers and kids aged about 2 and up. Chomper the purple dinosaur drives his steam train from station to station, and little ones tap the waiting animals - a cow, a pig, a giraffe, a penguin, Lenny the Lemur and more - to help them hop aboard, then press the big whistle to set off.

There's nothing to read, no timer and no way to lose. Every tap gets a cheerful sound, a little hand shows what to do after a quiet moment, and the ride rolls on by itself through tunnels and over bridges to the farm, the zoo, the beach and the snow. Best held sideways.

Free, no ads and no sign-up. More like this: train games and animal games.
