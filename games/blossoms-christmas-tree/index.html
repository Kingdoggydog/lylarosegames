About Blossom's Christmas Tree

Blossom's Christmas Tree is a free Christmas tree decorating game for kids aged about 2 and up, made with toddlers in mind. Children choose a tree, tap baubles, tinsel, fairy lights, candy canes and friendly animal ornaments onto the branches, pop a star or unicorn horn on top, then flick the big switch to light it up and open the presents underneath.

Everything works with one finger: no reading, no timer, no scores and nothing to get wrong. The twinkling is slow and gentle, there's a pastel colour option, and each finished tree stays saved on this device for next time.

Free, no ads and no sign-up. More like this: Christmas games and toddler games.
