About Blossom's Shape Sorter

Blossom's Shape Sorter is a free shape sorter game for toddlers aged about 2 and up. Like the classic wooden toy, children drag chunky circles, squares, triangles, stars, hearts and moons into the matching holes of a box while Blossom the Bunnycorn claps along.

Each shape and its hole share a colour, so little ones can match by colour or by shape. There's no reading, no timer and no losing - a shape in the wrong spot just boings back, and a tap makes the right hole glow.

Free, no ads and no sign-up. More like this: puzzle games and unicorn games.
