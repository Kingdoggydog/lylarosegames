About Trick-or-Treat Maze

Trick-or-Treat Maze is a free Halloween maze game for kids aged about 3 and up. Children pick Sparklehoof, Chomper or Lenny in a dress-up costume and wander a cosy little town at dusk, knocking on glowing doors where friendly neighbours in costumes hand out treats, before finding the Halloween party house.

It's gentle and never scary: smiling pumpkins, giggling bats and a helpful little ghost who shows the way. There's no timer and no losing, the lit-up pumpkins show where you've been, and progress saves on the device. Six levels to explore.

Free, no ads and no sign-up. More like this: Halloween games and maze games.
