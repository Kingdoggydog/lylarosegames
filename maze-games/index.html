Free maze games for kids

Our maze games are made for young kids who are just learning to find their way. Each maze starts small and grows a little with every level, so children build confidence before the bigger puzzles. They play online in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, with swipe, tap or arrow-key controls.

Every maze has its own twist. In Sparklehoof's Maze a unicorn gallops through a hedge maze collecting stars. In Chomper's Digger Maze kids drive a big yellow digger and dig straight through soft dirt walls for shortcuts. In Whirly's Cloud Maze a rescue helicopter blows the clouds away to uncover a hidden maze and fly animal friends to safety. And in Lenny's Jungle Maze Lenny the Lemur finds lost baby lemurs, who follow him home in a wobbly line, and swings across the river on jungle vines. For Halloween, Trick-or-Treat Maze sends kids round a cosy little town in costume, knocking on glowing doors for treats.

Mazes are great for practising directions, planning ahead and problem solving. There are no timers and nobody can get stuck - a hint button shows the way when little ones need a nudge. Looking for more brain games? Try our puzzle games.

Questions parents ask

What age can kids start doing mazes?

From about 3. Lenny's Jungle Maze, Chomper's Digger Maze and the Trick-or-Treat Maze start small and simple; Whirly's Cloud Maze and Sparklehoof's Maze are for 4 and up.

How do you play the mazes on a phone or tablet?

Swipe, or tap the big arrow buttons on the screen. On a computer, use the arrow keys or WASD.

Do the mazes get harder?

Yes - most have levels that grow bigger and twistier as you go, with things to collect along the way. All free, no ads.
