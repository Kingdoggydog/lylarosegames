Free maze games for kids

Our maze games are made for young kids who are just learning to find their way. Each maze starts small and grows a little with every level, so children build confidence before the bigger puzzles. They play online in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, with swipe, tap or arrow-key controls.

Every maze has its own twist. In Sparklehoof's Maze a unicorn gallops through a hedge maze collecting stars. In Chomper's Digger Maze kids drive a big yellow digger and dig straight through soft dirt walls for shortcuts. In Whirly's Cloud Maze a rescue helicopter blows the clouds away to uncover a hidden maze and fly animal friends to safety.

Mazes are great for practising directions, planning ahead and problem solving. There are no timers and nobody can get stuck - a hint button shows the way when little ones need a nudge. Looking for more brain games? Try our puzzle games.
