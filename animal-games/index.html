Free animal games for kids

Animal games starring a cheeky ring-tailed lemur, a friendly purple dinosaur, baby unicorns, ducklings, lambs, koalas, piglets and kittens. Catch bananas, rescue stuck friends, deliver Easter eggs and match jungle animal cards.

Try Lenny's Banana Catch, Whirlybird Rescue, Blossom's Easter Eggs, Lenny's Memory Match, Whirly's Cloud Maze, where you fly animal friends to a picnic, or Lenny's Jungle Maze, where baby lemurs follow Lenny home through the jungle. Puppy lovers can feed, bath, brush and dress up Biscuit the cavoodle in Biscuit's Puppy Care, then take him to the park in Biscuit's Fetch - he doesn't always bring back the ball! Little ones can also help a cow, a pig and a giraffe aboard Chomper's Animal Train, post shapes with Blossom and her friends in Blossom's Shape Sorter, or pop Lenny's bubbles to find fish, butterflies and baby lemurs inside in Lenny's Bubble Pop. At Halloween, Blossom's animal friends wander past her porch in little costumes in Blossom's Pumpkin Faces.

Every animal game is free, kind and never scary, and plays online on phones, tablets and computers with no sign-up.

Questions parents ask

Which animal games are there?

Biscuit the cavoodle puppy stars in Biscuit's Fetch and Biscuit's Puppy Care, Lenny the Lemur pops bubbles, catches bananas and finds his babies in a jungle maze, and Chomper's Animal Train takes cows, ducks and giraffes for a ride.

Which animal games suit the youngest?

The 2+ games: Biscuit's Puppy Care, Biscuit's Fetch, Lenny's Bubble Pop and Chomper's Animal Train are all taps, with no reading and no losing.

Are they free?

Yes - every animal game is free, with no ads and no sign-up, and plays in the browser on a tablet, phone or computer.
