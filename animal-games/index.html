Free animal games for kids

Animal games starring a cheeky ring-tailed lemur, a friendly purple dinosaur, baby unicorns, ducklings, lambs, koalas, piglets and kittens. Catch bananas, rescue stuck friends, deliver Easter eggs and match jungle animal cards.

Try Lenny's Banana Catch, Whirlybird Rescue, Blossom's Easter Eggs, Lenny's Memory Match or Whirly's Cloud Maze, where you fly animal friends to a picnic.

Every animal game is free, kind and never scary, and plays online on phones, tablets and computers with no sign-up.
