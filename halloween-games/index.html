Free Halloween games for kids

These Halloween games are made for little kids, so they're cosy rather than spooky. Think smiling jack-o'-lanterns, fairy lights, friendly bats and a little ghost who says hello - no monsters, no jump-scares and nothing that goes bump in the night. They play free in the browser on phones, tablets and computers.

In Trick-or-Treat Maze kids pick Sparklehoof the unicorn dressed as a witch, Chomper the dinosaur dressed as a pumpkin, or Lenny the Lemur in a ghost sheet, then go trick-or-treating through a little town maze. They knock on the glowing doors to fill their bucket with treats, light up pumpkins along the path and find the Halloween party house. It suits kids aged about 3 and up, with a hint button and no way to lose.

For toddlers there's Blossom's Pumpkin Faces - pumpkin carving with no knives. Little ones pick a pumpkin from Blossom the Bunnycorn's patch, tap on eyes, noses, hats and moustaches, then light it up and watch it giggle and dance. Every finished pumpkin joins the row on Blossom's porch. It's all one-finger tapping with nothing to read, made for kids aged about 2 and up.

Looking for something to play all year round? Try our maze games and puzzle games.

Questions parents ask

Are the Halloween games scary?

Not at all - they are cosy and friendly, with smiling pumpkins, dress-up costumes and glowing doors with treats behind them.

What ages are they for?

Blossom's Pumpkin Faces is for 2 and up (just tap to decorate). The Trick-or-Treat Maze suits 3 and up.

Can we play them after Halloween?

Of course - they stay free to play all year, with no ads and no sign-up.
