Free sport games for kids

Simple sport games for young kids: take penalty kicks against Lenny the Lemur in goal, or race Starlight the unicorn against her friends on sports day. Big buttons and easy controls make them perfect for little hands.

Unicorn Penalty Shoot-out is a soccer game where you aim and kick to score as many goals as you can. Starlight's Unicorn Race is a running race - tap to gallop, jump the hurdles and win a medal (there's never a "you lose" screen).

Both games are free, play online in the browser and work on phones, tablets and computers.

Questions parents ask

What sport games are there?

Unicorn Penalty Shoot-out - aim, kick and score past Lenny the Lemur - and Starlight's Unicorn Race, a hurdles race against Lenny, Blossom and Chomper.

What age are they for?

The unicorn race suits 3 and up (tap to gallop). The penalty shoot-out is for 4 and up, with a score to beat.

Do they work on a phone?

Yes - both are simple taps on a phone or tablet, or the space bar on a computer. Free, no ads.
