Free games for 2 and 3 year olds

These are the simplest games on the site, made for toddlers who are just learning to tap and drag. There are no words to read, no timers and no way to lose - every tap does something fun, and a little celebration is never far away. They were tested by a two-year-old who loves trains.

Pop bubbles full of surprises in Lenny's Bubble Pop, throw the ball for a cavoodle puppy in Biscuit's Fetch, or feed, bath and dress him up in Biscuit's Puppy Care. Little train fans can toot along in Chomper's Choo-Choo Express and help animals hop aboard in Chomper's Animal Train.

For first shapes and colours there's Blossom's Shape Sorter, which works just like the wooden toy. Chomper's Car Crunch lets a friendly purple dinosaur stomp and crunch cartoon cars into bouncing bits, and Blossom's Pumpkin Faces is a cosy, not-scary Halloween game for decorating pumpkins. At Christmas they can decorate a tree, switch on the twinkly lights and open presents in Blossom's Christmas Tree.

Every game is free, with no ads and no sign-ups, and plays straight in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Most are happiest on a tablet, where little fingers have more room. When your toddler is ready for a bit more, try our games for 4 year olds, animal games or easy maze games.

Questions parents ask

What makes a game good for a 2 year old?

Big things to tap, no words to read, and no way to lose. Every game on this page works with a single tap or a simple drag, and something fun happens every time.

Which game should we try first?

Lenny's Bubble Pop or Biscuit's Fetch - a single tap does something fun, so even the youngest players get it straight away. Train fans usually love Chomper's Animal Train.

Can my toddler play without me?

Most can after a turn or two together. There are no ads or pop-ups to tap by accident, no links out of the games and nothing to buy, so it is hard to end up anywhere you didn't expect.
