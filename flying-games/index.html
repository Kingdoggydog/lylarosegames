Free flying games for kids

Up, up and away! These flying games let kids soar through the sky with a flying unicorn or a friendly rescue helicopter. Steering is simple - drag, tap or use the arrow keys - so even young children can fly.

In Glitter Sky Unicorn you fly Rainbow across the sky catching glitter. In Whirlybird Rescue you fly Whirly the helicopter with Lenny the Lemur to rescue animal friends stuck up trees and on rooftops. And Whirly's Cloud Maze is a helicopter maze game hidden under the clouds.

All free to play online with no ads or downloads, on phones, tablets and computers.

Questions parents ask

What flying games are there?

Glitter Sky Unicorn (fly a unicorn and catch glitter), Whirlybird Rescue (rescue animals in a helicopter) and Whirly's Cloud Maze (a helicopter maze hidden under the clouds).

How do kids fly on a tablet or phone?

By dragging a finger or tapping - on a computer the arrow keys work too. Glitter Sky and Whirlybird Rescue suit 3 and up; the cloud maze is a bit trickier, for 4 and up.

Are the flying games free?

Yes - all free, with no ads and no sign-up.
