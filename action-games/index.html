Free action games for kids

Action games for young kids: quick, bright and easy to pick up, without anything scary. Catch falling bananas, dodge and jump in a runner game, smash toy cars as a friendly dinosaur, play pinball, or drive a steam train.

Favourites include Glitter Getaway, Chomper's Car Crunch, Unicorn Pinball and Chomper's Choo-Choo Express. Most action games have a best score saved on your device, so kids can try to beat it next time. For the very youngest, Lenny's Bubble Pop is the gentlest of all - tap the big slow bubbles and see what pops out - and in Biscuit's Fetch one tap throws the ball for a bouncy cavoodle puppy.

Free to play online with no ads, on phones, tablets and computers.

Questions parents ask

Are the action games OK for little kids?

Yes - "action" here means gentle catching, jumping, racing and popping, never anything scary. Lenny's Bubble Pop and Biscuit's Fetch suit 2 year olds.

Which action games are the most challenging?

Unicorn Pinball, Lenny's Banana Catch and Glitter Getaway need quicker fingers and good timing - best for 4 and up.

Are they free?

Yes - all free, with no ads or sign-up, on phones, tablets and computers.
