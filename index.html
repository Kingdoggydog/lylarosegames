About Lyla Rose Games

Lyla Rose Games is a collection of free online games for kids aged about 2 to 6. Every game runs straight in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer - there's nothing to download, no sign-up and no ads, so little ones can tap a picture and start playing.

The games were made by a dad for his daughter Lyla, and lots of them started as her ideas. You'll find unicorn games, maze games, train games, digger games, dinosaur games, helicopter rescues, jigsaws and memory games, starring a friendly cast of characters like Starlight the unicorn, Lenny the Lemur and Chomper the purple dinosaur.

Each game has an age badge (2+, 3+ or 4+) to help you pick, and the Age buttons at the top show one group at a time. Most games have no way to lose - just stars, stickers and happy celebrations. Find out more on the For parents page.

Made with love. More games on the way.
