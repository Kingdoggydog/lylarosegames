About Lyla Rose Games

Lyla Rose Games is a collection of free online games for kids aged about 2 to 6. Every game runs straight in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer - there's nothing to download, no sign-up and no ads, so little ones can tap a picture and start playing.

The games are made by a dad for his little girl Lyla and her toddler brother, and lots of them started as their ideas. You'll find easy toddler games, train games, digger games, dinosaur games, animal games, maze games, unicorn games, not-scary Halloween games, helicopter rescues, jigsaws and memory games, starring a friendly cast of characters like Chomper the purple dinosaur, Biscuit the puppy, Lenny the Lemur and Starlight the unicorn.

Each game has an age badge (2+, 3+ or 4+) to help you pick, and the Age buttons at the top show one group at a time. Most games have no way to lose - just stars, stickers and happy celebrations. Find out more on the For parents page.

Questions parents ask

Are the games really free?

Yes. Every game on Lyla Rose Games is free to play, with no ads, no in-game shop and no sign-up. Just tap a game and it starts.

What ages are the games for?

Mostly kids aged about 2 to 6. Every card has an age badge (2+, 3+ or 4+), and there are pages to help you pick: toddler games for 2 and 3 year olds, games for 4 year olds and games for 5 and 6 year olds.

Do they work on an iPad, tablet or phone?

Yes - the games play straight in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers, with nothing to download. On a phone or tablet you can add the site to your home screen so it opens like an app.

Is it safe for my child to play on their own?

The site has no ads, no chat, no sign-ups and no links out of the games, and it never asks for personal details. Progress and favourites are saved only on your device. The For parents page has the details.
