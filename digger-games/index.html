Free digger games for kids

Digger and construction games for kids who love big machines. Scoop dirt with a yellow digger, fill Tipper the dump truck, flatten roads with Rolla the steamroller and build a whole little town.

Play Chomper's Digger, Busy Building Site or Chomper's Digger Maze, where you dig through dirt walls to find treasure and dinosaur bones.

No timers and no losing - just digging, building and lots of beeping. Free to play online on phones, tablets and computers.

Questions parents ask

Which digger game should we start with?

Chomper's Digger - dig with the big yellow digger, fill up Tipper the dump truck, find buried treasure and build things like a duck pond and a train tunnel.

What else is there for digger fans?

In Busy Building Site kids drive a digger, tipper, bulldozer and steamroller to build a little town, and Chomper's Digger Maze lets them dig through dirt walls for shortcuts.

What age are the digger games for?

About 3 and up. They use drag or tap on a tablet, or the arrow keys on a computer, and they are free with no ads.
