Free train games for kids

All aboard! Train games for toddlers and young kids who love steam trains. Toot the whistle, stop at the platforms and pick up passengers, or switch the tracks on a toy train set to deliver milk, toys and bananas.

Chomper's Choo-Choo Express is a simple driving game - hold to go, let go to stop at the station. Chomper's Toot Toot Delivery is a gentle train puzzle where you flick the switches to send the train to the right station. For the youngest, Chomper's Animal Train is all taps - help the cows, ducks and giraffes hop aboard, toot the whistle and ride through tunnels to the next stop.

All three are free to play online on phones, tablets and computers, with no ads and no sign-up.

Questions parents ask

Which train game is best for a toddler?

Chomper's Animal Train is all taps - help the animals aboard and toot the whistle. Chomper's Choo-Choo Express adds a little driving: hold to go and let go to stop at the station.

Is there a train game for older kids?

Yes - Chomper's Toot Toot Delivery (4+) is a gentle puzzle where you flick the switches on a toy train set to send each delivery to the right station.

Do the train games work on a tablet?

Yes - all three play in the browser on tablets, phones and computers, with big buttons for little fingers. They are free, with no ads and no sign-up.
